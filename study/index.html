Standard Reference Lab Ranges & Clinical Pointers

Critical cutoff values and quick diagnostic associations for high-velocity exam scenarios.

Test / Analyte Standard Range Units High-Yield Clinical Pearl
Serum Sodium (Na+) 135 – 145 mEq/L Correct slow (< 8-10 mEq/24h) to avoid Osmotic Demyelination Syndrome (ODS).
Serum Potassium (K+) 3.5 – 5.0 mEq/L K+ > 6.0 mEq/L with peaked T-waves: First step is IV Calcium Gluconate (cardioprotection).
Serum Calcium 8.5 – 10.5 mg/dL Correct for albumin: Corrected Ca = Measured Ca + 0.8 × (4.0 - Albumin).
Arterial Blood pH 7.35 – 7.45 pH units Anion Gap = Na - (Cl + HCO3). Normal is 8-12. Use MUDPILES for elevated gap.
Serum Creatinine 0.6 – 1.2 mg/dL BUN:Cr > 20:1 suggests prerenal azotemia; < 15:1 indicates intrinsic renal acute tubular necrosis.
Troponin I (High-Sensitivity) < 0.04 ng/mL Rises at 3-6 hrs, peaks 12-24 hrs, persists 7-10 days. Check CK-MB for re-infarction.
D-Dimer < 500 ng/mL FEU High sensitivity, low specificity. Excellent negative predictive value for low-risk PE/DVT.

High-Yield Emergency Decision Trees

First-line interventions and emergency checklists essential for clinical OSCE and viva stations.

⚡ Acute Anaphylaxis Protocol

  1. First Line: IM Adrenaline (Epinephrine) 1:1,000, 0.5 mg (0.5 mL) anterolateral mid-thigh. Repeat at 5 min if no response.
  2. Airway & Breathing: High-flow oxygen (15 L/min non-rebreather mask).
  3. Circulation: 2x wide-bore IV cannulae (14G/16G) + rapid fluid bolus 1000 mL warmed crystalloid.
  4. Adjuncts: IV Hydrocortisone (200 mg) + IV Chlorpheniramine (10 mg).
  5. Refractory: Start IV adrenaline infusion, prepare for endotracheal intubation or cricothyroidotomy.

🩸 Diabetic Ketoacidosis (DKA) Protocol

  1. Fluids: 0.9% Normal Saline 1 L/hr initially. Add 5% Dextrose once Blood Glucose < 250 mg/dL (14 mmol/L).
  2. Potassium Check: If K+ < 3.3, HOLD insulin, give KCl first. If K+ 3.3–5.3, add 20-30 mEq/L fluid + start insulin.
  3. Insulin: Regular insulin IV infusion 0.1 units/kg/hr. Target glucose fall of 50-75 mg/dL/hr.
  4. Resolution criteria: Anion gap normalizes (< 12), venous pH > 7.3, HCO3 > 18. Transition to subcutaneous 2 hrs before stopping IV.
